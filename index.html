The Meaning of Sayla Lumina

Sayla Lumina was named for Sayla Marie Yates-Fogos as a symbol of the light she carries with her everywhere she goes. Her bright soul, unforgettable personality, warmth and love have a way of making the world around her feel a little brighter.

So a place in the night sky was chosen to carry her name. Sayla Lumina rests in the constellation Leo at right ascension 11h 44m 30.66s and declination +14° 36′ 32.18″, with a listed magnitude of 9.6719.

Just as Sayla's light makes the lives around her brighter, Sayla Lumina is meant to be a reminder that her brightness has a place among the stars — shining on, night after night, as something beautiful that will burn forever.

It is a small piece of the sky for someone whose presence feels anything but small.
